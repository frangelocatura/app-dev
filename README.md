# My Favorite Series: The Walking Dead

![The Walking Dead](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/The-Walking-Dead-The-Poster-Collection/AMC/Insights-Poster-Collections/9781608872251)

## About
**The Walking Dead** is a *post-apocalyptic horror drama* that premiered on **AMC** on **October 31, 2010**. It is based on the comic book series by Robert Kirkman, Tony Moore, and Charlie Adlard, and it ran for **11 seasons**.

> "We are the walking dead."
> — Rick Grimes

---

## Main Characters
1. **Rick Grimes** – a sheriff's deputy who wakes up from a coma and becomes the group's leader
2. **Daryl Dixon** – a skilled tracker and crossbow user
3. **Michonne** – a sword-wielding survivor and fierce protector
4. **Carol Peletier** – a survivor who grows from timid to tough
5. **Negan** – the charismatic and ruthless leader of the Saviors

## Why I Like It
- Intense and unpredictable storyline
- Memorable characters
  - Strong character development
  - Tough moral choices
- Shows that the **real monsters** are often the living
- ~~Boring episodes~~ (hardly any!)
